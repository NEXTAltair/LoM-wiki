# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

The great-grandfather of [[Ye Yun Zhou]] and [[Ye Yunshang]] was one of the trusted generals under Yue Yun, son of the famous anti-Jin general Wu Muwang, Yue Fei.

<br>

It is said that Emperor Gaozong issued twelve gold plaques recalling him, and Yue Fei, loyal to the end, willingly sacrificed himself — but lamented that the anti-Jin cause remained unfinished, fearing the empire would be helpless if his military power fell into the wrong hands. So he dispersed the main force of the Yue Army and hid its treasure in the mountains, entrusting an iron tally to his eldest son Yue Yun, hoping that one day the tally could command the old troops again and carry on the mission of protecting the country.

<br>

Yue Yun, unexpectedly, died before his father. In his final crisis, he entrusted the tally to a subordinate, instructing him to hide in the martial world and wait for a descendant of Wu Muwang to come claim it. That subordinate — the Ye family's own ancestor — concealed his identity and lived in the mountains, and for this the family even gave up their own surname, taking the name "Ye Yun" to honor the lord their ancestor had served; even the family's heirloom sword, "Listening Cloud," means "obeying Yue Yun."

## A branch that broke away: Yeli Chahan

Not every descendant of the old Yue Army kept that vow. The ancestors of Li Yuanqi (born Yeli Chahan), also descended from the same Yue Army veterans, lost heart after Yue Fei's death and, seeking revenge, defected to Western Xia. That branch later rose to become the Left Enforcer of the [[Blissful Sect]], defeating Qingcheng's [[Zhao Kui]] during [[The Battle of Ji Le Teachings]] before fleeing wounded, and resurfacing years later under a new name.

## The secret comes to light, and the Ye family falls

The secret was kept for nearly a hundred years — but it eventually became known to others, and the treasure and tally brought disaster. Fearing the tally would fall into the wrong hands, [[Ye Yunshang]]'s parents branded it onto their beloved daughter's back before destroying the tally itself.

<br>

Not long after, the Baifengdong Rebellion broke out. The Ye family reported their own kinsman Li Yuanqi to the court, hoping to ruin his reputation — but the court turned on them instead, and the family was wiped out. [[Ye Yun Zhou]] and [[Ye Yunshang]] were left orphans.

## Li Yuanqi's atonement

Homeless, the Ye siblings traveled south to seek shelter with their kinsman Li Yuanqi — only to be turned away without mercy. In truth, he feared implicating them and wanted to preserve the Ye family's name as loyal subjects. That night he slipped out of camp alone, found [[Ye Yunshang]] near death, and fed her the "Nine Springs Pill" to save her life, urging her to value her own life over duty and honor.

<br>

In the end, Li Yuanqi fell to [[Tang Buyi]] and four other heroes, dying in the closing days of the Baifengdong Rebellion.
