# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

Xiahou, the head of the Snow Mountain Sect, had a deep friendship with the head of the Shangguan family, [[Shang Guan Zhun]], and before [[Xiahou Lan]] was even born, the two had already arranged a betrothal between their unborn children.

<br>

It all started with an insignificant argument. One day, disciples of the Snow Mountain Sect encountered people from the Kongtong Sect at an inn. The Kongtong disciples teased that Kongtong was colder in winter than the Snow Mountain Sect, leading to an argument and a fight. With blades and swords having no eyes, someone was accidentally killed, and both sides became bloodthirsty, seeking more people to settle the score, escalating the conflict.

<br>

The Snow Mountain Sect sought help from the [[Shangguan Family]], but [[Shang Guan Zhun|the head of the Shangguan family]] used the excuse of a distant journey to coldly watch from the sidelines. In the end, the Snow Mountain Sect was exterminated by the [[Kongtong Sect]]; the only daughter of the sect leader, [[Xiahou Lan|Xiahou Mei]], was taken away, renamed [[Xiahou Lan]], and became the successor disciple of Kongtong's Soul-Stealing Sect.

## Aftermath

The culprit from the Feitian Sect died in the chaos of the battle on the mountain. The one who killed the sect leader had their head twisted off by [[Xiahou Lan]] once she reached great martial prowess, and their corpse was hung up at Banyuping to dry into a mummy — her father's death, avenged.

<br>

Everything at the site was burned by fire and buried under heavy snow; nothing remained but five large rocks of the Plum Blossom Stake and a single snow tree. The disciple [[Di San Xiang|Third Xiang]] escaped the massacre and later reunited with [[Xiahou Lan]] at Kongtong Mountain — a story for another time.

<br>

The betrothal between the two families quietly died along with the Snow Mountain Sect.

## Notes

- In the game, the Great Snow Mountain is located near Kongtong Mountain in Gansu, far from the real Snow Mountain range in Sichuan. Niaoxiong has said it is purely a reference, not meant to be geographically accurate.[^1]
- Following [[Xiahou Lan]]'s storyline, [[Zhao Huo]] becomes the senior disciple of the Snow Mountain Sect.
- In a 2025 fan-vote event ("Send New Year Cards to Your Sweetheart"), the developer released a follow-up story: under [[Xiahou Lan]]'s leadership, the Snow Mountain Sect rose again to become one of the seven great sects of the martial world, its disciples spread across the land.[^2]
